Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers OddlyClear Uploader ("the tool"), a desktop tool that OddlyClear's creator uses to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Who uses the tool

Only the OddlyClear creator, on their own computer, for their own accounts. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • Sign-in tokens. When the creator connects an account through the platform's own sign-in page (Google, Facebook/Instagram or TikTok), the platform gives the tool an access token. The tool never sees or stores a password.
  • Basic account information needed to show which account a video goes to: the YouTube channel, the Instagram username and connected Facebook Page, or the TikTok display name.
  • Video details for videos the tool uploads: the video's ID, link, processing status and thumbnail, to confirm the upload worked.

The tool does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics or any other account data, and it never accesses other people's accounts.

How the data is used and stored

Tokens and upload records are stored only in files on the creator's own computer. They are used only to upload the creator's videos and check the result. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform the video is being posted to (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok). No data is sold, shared, used for advertising or analytics, or given to third parties.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the creator's computer until the creator deletes them or revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings: Google account → Security → Third-party connections; Facebook → Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; deleting the files removes them.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone other than the creator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
