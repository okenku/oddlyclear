OddlyClear

Short, funny science explainers made with Roblox characters. Why do we get brain freeze? Why does time feel faster as you get older? Answered in under a minute.

Where to watch

OddlyClear posts Shorts on YouTube, Reels on Instagram (@oddly_clearshow) and videos on TikTok.

OddlyClear Uploader

OddlyClear Uploader is a small desktop tool the channel's creator uses to publish their own finished videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It runs only on the creator's computer and is not offered to other people.

  • YouTube: uploads a finished video with its title, description and thumbnail.
  • Instagram: publishes a finished video as a Reel with its caption.
  • TikTok: sends a finished video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator adds the caption and posts it themselves in the TikTok app.

Each platform is connected through that platform's own sign-in page; the tool never sees a password.
